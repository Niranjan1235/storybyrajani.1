Good to know

Questions, answered

How early should we book?

As early as you can. Wedding dates fill up quickly, so we recommend getting in touch as soon as your date is fixed.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes. We cover weddings across Hyderabad, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh and beyond. Share your location when you enquire and we will confirm the details.

Do you shoot both photos and films?

We offer wedding photography and cinematic films together or separately, along with pre-wedding shoots, events, corporate shoots and portraits.

How long until we receive our photos and film?

Timelines depend on the event and what you have booked. We will share a clear estimate when we confirm your date.

How do we confirm our date?

Send an enquiry with your name, number, event type and date. We will call you to discuss the next steps.
